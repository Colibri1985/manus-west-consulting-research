# West Consulting — Research Revision Package

**Prepared:** 4 October 2026  
**Purpose:** evidence‑controlled revision of the original desk research and decision materials.

## How to use this package

1. Start with the [Executive Decision Memo](apps/L_executive_decision_memo.md) and [Updated Decisions](updated_decisions.md).
2. Use the market/pricing/channel appendices to challenge or refine the commercial model.
3. Treat all recommendations and hypotheses as tests, not facts.
4. Before publishing, implementing lead forms or making legal claims, follow the [Research Gap Register](research_gap_register.md) and relevant review gates.

## Control documents

- [Central Source Log](source_log.md) — source URL, access date, geography, type, reliability and proof limits.
- [Research Gap Register](research_gap_register.md) — unknowns, next evidence and decision unlocked.
- [Updated Decisions](updated_decisions.md) — consolidated decision changes and gates.

## Evidence labels

- **ФАКТ:** directly supported by the cited source.
- **ОЦЕНКА:** analyst’s interpretation of evidence.
- **ГИПОТЕЗА:** a proposition requiring validation.
- **РЕКОМЕНДАЦИЯ:** proposed action, not an empirical claim.
- **RESEARCH GAP:** explicitly missing evidence.
