## 2. Roadmap by horizon

| Horizon | Must be ready | Can be partner‑provided | Must not be claimed yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before launch | Qualification, paid entry scope, CRM/privacy baseline, basic cash/reporting templates, referrals/no‑fit rules. | Legal review, website implementation, CRM configuration. | Large team, software delivery, case results, AI transformation. |
| 0–90 days | Diagnostic workflow, one core service method, proposal/change control, content/research hygiene. | 1С/BI pilot discovery, designer/editor. | Repeatable partner channel, KPI of content, mature retainer. |
| 3–6 months | External CFO cadence only if evidence/capacity support it; case permission system. | RPA/AI pilot technical work, sector specialists. | Formal partnership/industry specialization without evidence. |
| 6–12 months | Repeatable delivery playbook, role handover, sector focus if validated. | Support finance manager, technical delivery teams. | Autonomous AI, full implementation house, enterprise scale without operations. |

## 3. Capability verification gate

A capability can be named publicly only when all applicable conditions are met:

1. **Evidence:** documented experience, training, recognised qualification or reviewed work product.
2. **Repeatability:** a process/template exists; it is not one improvised example.
3. **Boundary:** owner knows what is excluded and when to refer.
4. **Delivery capacity:** time, backup/partner and escalation are defined.
5. **Legal/security:** appropriate basis and controls exist for client data.
6. **Proof wording:** claim has an auditable source and does not overstate individual/team role.

**РЕКОМЕНДАЦИЯ:** Until the gate is passed, use future‑safe language such as “организуем совместную работу с профильным интегратором” rather than “внедряем/автоматизируем под ключ”.
