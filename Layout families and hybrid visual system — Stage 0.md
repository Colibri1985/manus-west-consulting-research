# Layout families and hybrid visual system — Stage 0

## Approved direction

**«Карта решений + Перспектива»**. The concept images are art direction, not raster page backgrounds or ready-made interfaces.

Core visual grammar:

- dark deep-navy spatial hero on homepage;
- light ivory/stone reading surfaces for content;
- copper rules and restrained blue diagram nodes;
- serif for meaning and high-level narrative;
- sans-serif for navigation, forms, tables and operational explanation;
- a small static decision map: owner question → financial picture → data/rules → roles/processes → next decision;
- technology is a supporting layer; AI is not a mandatory branch of every client problem.

## Families

### A. Homepage / perspective

- left-aligned problem and approved working copy;
- right-side spatial decision map with accessible HTML labels and SVG/CSS links;
- four numbered situation rows as the first substantive section;
- founder block earlier than the end of the page;
- service formats and controlled automation lower in the hierarchy;
- no required hover, animation or quiz before contact.

### B. Service detail

Used for financial diagnostic, finance under control, external CFO and finance automation.

- compact hero;
- audience and fit;
- scope and results;
- process/cadence;
- boundaries and exclusions;
- FAQ;
- one contextual CTA;
- calculator only where relevant, never as a mandatory barrier.

### C. Scenario detail

- demonstrational label visible above or near H1;
- context and symptoms;
- questions before solution;
- possible order of work;
- example outputs clearly labelled as possible/demonstrational;
- roles of owner, West and partner;
- no-fit and boundaries;
- next step to relevant service/contact.

### D. Editorial

- no cinematic full-screen hero;
- category, title, date, approved author, calculated reading time;
- short conclusion;
- body, sources and limitations;
- related scenario/service;
- one primary CTA.

### E. Personal / approach

- real founder portrait, approved identity and role only;
- method, responsibility, boundaries, partner handoff;
- readable process map, not a decorative abstract hero.

### F. Action/tool

- contact: minimum decoration and safe-material warning;
- calculator: explicit demo state and public-disabled state before tariff approval;
- Cash Risk Check: 12 questions, ungated, no diagnosis or forced contact;
- navigator: selected state, keyboard/mobile support and scenario detail links.

## Design decisions already fixed

- Keep CFO as the core; AI and digital expertise are supporting capabilities.
- Do not turn the site into a generic AI/architecture brand.
- Do not use the generated concept images as page screenshots or as a single raster interface.
- Use separate mobile compositions: text/CTA before map, simplified connections, vertical process steps.
